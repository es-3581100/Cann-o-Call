## What this means for you

### Small organizations: free internal use allowed

If you operate a small organization with **fewer than 50 full-time-equivalent
employees AND less than US $5,000,000 aggregate gross annual revenue**, you
may use Cann-o-Call internally for production purposes without paying a
commercial license fee. Both thresholds are aggregated across your organization
and all Affiliates.

This grant covers:

- Your own internal business use
- Internal organizational use
- Internal operational use
- Qualifying nonprofit internal use
- Teaching
- Coursework
- Academic research
- Qualifying educational institutional use

### Enterprise/SaaS/MSP/resale/white-label: may require commercial license

The following uses require a separate commercial license:

- Hosted SaaS or hosted Cann-o-Call service for third parties
- Managed services or managed infrastructure sold to third parties
- Paid automation or agent operations for third parties
- Resale, sublicensing as a commercial offering, or rental or lease of access
- White-label offerings
- Commercial services where Cann-o-Call functionality forms a material part

These uses are excluded from the free Additional Use Grant and require a
separate commercial license.

### Nonprofit and educational institutions

Nonprofits and educational institutions may qualify for free internal use **if they meet the same financial thresholds** (aggregate revenue < $5M, < 50 FTE). However, they still must abide by the same restrictions around SaaS/managed services/resale/white-label.

### Affiliate aggregation

Eligibility is calculated across the user organization and all **Affiliates**. An Affiliate is any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with the user organization.

If combined across all Affiliates you exceed either threshold, you become
non-qualifying and require a commercial license.

### Grace period for previously qualifying users

If you were previously qualifying (met all criteria at some point) and later exceed either threshold, you may continue your existing permitted deployment for **up to 30 days** primarily to either:

- Obtain a commercial license, OR
- Discontinue the newly non-permitted use

During the grace period, you may not materially expand the deployment or create
new deployments relying on the grant.
